# Speech Dictation in the Launcher

The Launcher comes with a speech dictation surface that transcribes your voice using Apple's speech recognition. You can review, edit, copy, or paste the result directly from the Launcher.

## Starting Dictation

From the main results list, select **Start Dictation** and press `↩`. This expands the Launcher into the dictation surface and begins recording immediately.

You can also make dictation more discoverable by:

- Assigning it a custom **alias** (e.g. `dict`).
- Moving it to the **Commands** group (this is the default) and giving the group its own shortcut.
- Pinning it to the pills bar.

## Surface Behavior

While recording:

- A transcript builds up live in an editable text area.
- The footer shows recording status.
- You can pause/restart recording.

Available commands:

| Command | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Restart recording | `⌘R` |
| Copy transcript to clipboard | `⌘C` |
| Paste transcript into the previously focused app | `↩` |

When you leave the surface with `Esc`, recording stops.

## Permissions

You need to grant BetterTouchTool microphone access and speech recognition access the first time you use dictation. macOS will prompt automatically; if you denied permission earlier, re-enable it in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
